# Problem Statement

## Title

**Rising Waters: Flood Prediction Using Machine Learning**

---

## Background

Floods are one of the most destructive natural disasters, causing significant damage to human lives, agriculture, infrastructure, and the environment. Accurate flood prediction is essential for minimizing these losses by enabling timely warnings and preparedness.

Traditional flood forecasting methods often rely on manual observation or complex simulations that may be time-consuming and require extensive resources.

---

## Problem

Many regions lack an efficient, accessible, and intelligent system that can quickly predict flood risk using environmental parameters. Delayed or inaccurate predictions can reduce the time available for emergency response and disaster management.

---

## Proposed Solution

The Rising Waters SmartBridge project addresses this problem by developing a machine learning-based flood prediction system. Users can enter relevant environmental parameters through a web interface, and the trained model predicts the likelihood of flooding in real time.

The application is developed using Python, Flask, and Scikit-learn, providing a simple, fast, and user-friendly solution for flood risk prediction.

---

## Objectives

- Develop a machine learning model for flood prediction.
- Build a user-friendly web application using Flask.
- Predict flood risk based on environmental parameters.
- Improve decision-making by providing timely predictions.
- Demonstrate the application of AI in disaster management.

---

## Expected Outcome

The system provides quick and reliable flood risk predictions, helping users understand potential flood conditions and supporting better preparedness and disaster response.
